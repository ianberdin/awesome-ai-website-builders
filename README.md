# Awesome AI Website Builders

A curated list of AI-powered website builders that create, design, and host websites.

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

AI website builders turn a text prompt, a few questions, or an existing design into a live website. This list covers tools that generate complete sites, landing pages, online stores, and marketing pages, as well as design-to-code generators and AI features built into established website and CMS platforms. Every entry is a real, publicly available product, described in a single neutral sentence. Listing is not an endorsement.

## Prompt-to-Website / AI Site Generators

Tools that take a text prompt or a few questions and generate a complete, hostable website.

- [B12](https://www.b12.io) — AI website builder for professional-service businesses that generates a site in about a minute and adds scheduling, invoicing, and email marketing.
- [Butternut AI](https://www.butternut.ai) — AI website builder for small businesses that generates a complete site from a prompt with hosting, SSL, SEO, blogs, and chatbot features.
- [Chariot](https://chariotai.com) — AI web developer that builds and edits websites through a chat interface and handles publishing, hosting, SSL, and domains on paid plans.
- [CodeDesign.ai](https://codedesign.ai) — AI website builder that generates sites from a chat prompt or drag-and-drop editor and can host, sync to WordPress, or export code.
- [doany](https://doany.ai) — AI company builder that generates and hosts a business website and product from a plain-language idea, with Stripe payments and a free plan.
- [Dora](https://www.dora.run) — No-code AI website builder focused on 3D-animated and scroll-interactive sites, generating custom designs from a prompt.
- [Dorik AI](https://dorik.com) — No-code website builder that generates complete sites from text prompts and includes a drag-and-drop editor, CMS, and AI text and image generation.
- [Durable](https://durable.com) — AI website builder for small and service businesses that generates a complete site in about 30 seconds and bundles CRM, invoicing, and marketing tools.
- [GoDaddy Airo](https://www.godaddy.com/airo) — AI platform that creates websites and stores through conversation and bundles domain, logo, and marketing-content generation.
- [Hostinger AI Website Builder](https://www.hostinger.com/ai-website-builder) — AI builder that generates a website from a single prompt with integrated hosting.
- [Jimdo Dolphin](https://www.jimdo.com) — AI builder that generates a multi-page website in about three minutes by asking questions about the user's business.
- [Kleap](https://kleap.co) — AI website builder that generates deployable React sites from plain-language prompts, with domain, hosting, forms, analytics, SEO, and payments included.
- [Landingsite.ai](https://www.landingsite.ai) — AI website builder that generates a site with copy, images, layout, and a logo from a short business description, with hosting and a custom domain.
- [Lindo AI](https://lindo.ai) — White-label AI website builder for agencies that generates client websites from business data and includes client management, billing, and hosting.
- [Mixo](https://www.mixo.io) — AI builder that generates landing pages and small-business websites from a short text description, including copy, visuals, and lead-capture forms.
- [Mobirise AI](https://mobirise.com) — Website builder that generates multiple complete website versions from a text prompt, with hosting included.
- [Pineapple Builder](https://www.pineapplebuilder.com) — AI website builder that generates a business website from a description, with built-in SEO, analytics, and AI content tools.
- [Playcode](https://playcode.io/ai-website-builder) — AI website builder that creates, hosts, and updates sites from natural-language prompts, with custom domains and one-click publishing.
- [Popsy](https://popsy.co) — No-code, Notion-like website builder for creators, with templates, responsive design, SEO, and custom-domain publishing.
- [Readdy AI](https://readdy.ai) — No-code AI website builder that generates layouts, pages, copy, images, and structure from a prompt, image, or reference URL, with one-click publish.
- [Renderforest](https://www.renderforest.com/ai-website-builder) — Creative platform whose website builder generates responsive sites with AI-selected images and AI-generated copy.
- [SiteW](https://www.en.sitew.com) — Website builder with an AI assistant that generates sites from questions and can migrate an existing site from a URL.
- [Sitejet](https://www.sitejet.io/en) — Website builder for agencies and businesses with an AI generator that creates a complete one-page site from basic business details.
- [Sitekick](https://www.sitekick.ai) — AI landing-page and website builder that generates pages with copy and images from a description, plus a drag-and-drop editor and hosting.
- [Squarespace Blueprint AI](https://www.squarespace.com/websites/ai-website-builder) — AI builder, part of Squarespace's Design Intelligence suite, that generates a custom-designed site from brand inputs through guided choices.
- [Strikingly AI](https://www.strikingly.com/s/ai_site_builder) — Website builder whose AI generates a single- or multi-page site with text, images, and sections from a title and description.
- [Webador](https://www.webador.com/website-builder) — Website builder with an AI feature that generates a functional site from a description, aimed at small businesses and e-commerce.
- [Wegic](https://wegic.ai) — Chat-based AI website builder that generates multi-page websites from a text description, with editing, custom domains, and one-click publishing.
- [Wix AI Site Generator](https://www.wix.com/ai-website-builder) — AI flow that generates a full site from a set of questions, editable in the Wix drag-and-drop editor.

## AI Builders for Developers (Code Output)

Tools that generate editable code and often deploy full-stack web apps and sites.

- [Base44](https://base44.com) — No-code AI app builder, owned by Wix, that generates full-stack web apps with auth, database, and hosting from a conversational chat interface.
- [Bolt.new](https://bolt.new) — Browser-based tool from StackBlitz that generates, runs, edits, and deploys full-stack web apps from text prompts using WebContainers.
- [Create](https://www.create.xyz) — AI builder that turns text prompts into websites, apps, and tools with code, model integrations, and dozens of integrations.
- [Famous.ai](https://famous.ai) — AI app builder that turns prompts into web and mobile applications, including frontend, backend, database, and hosting.
- [Figma Make](https://www.figma.com/make) — Figma feature that turns prompts into responsive, interactive websites and web apps on the canvas, with a Supabase connection and web publishing.
- [Lazy AI](https://getlazy.ai) — No-code platform that builds full-stack web apps, AI agents, automations, and chatbots from prompts or templates and deploys them automatically.
- [Lovable](https://lovable.dev) — AI app builder that generates full-stack web applications from natural-language prompts, writing code and provisioning database and backend infrastructure.
- [MarsX](https://marsx.dev) — Open-source dev tool combining AI, no-code, and code via reusable microapps to build SaaS web and mobile applications.
- [Replit Agent](https://replit.com/products/agent) — AI agent inside Replit that generates and deploys full-stack web and mobile apps from natural-language prompts.
- [Rocket.new](https://www.rocket.new) — AI builder that turns prompts into websites and full-stack apps, generating frontend, backend, and database code.
- [Softgen](https://softgen.ai) — AI web-app builder that turns natural-language specs into full-stack Next.js and Firebase projects with version history, GitHub export, and Vercel deploy.
- [Taskade Genesis](https://www.taskade.com/wiki/genesis) — AI app builder that generates complete websites and business apps with databases, AI agents, and automations from a single prompt.
- [Tempo](https://www.tempo.new) — Visual IDE that uses AI to design, plan, and generate React web apps with live preview and code export.
- [Trickle AI](https://trickle.so) — AI agentic canvas that turns natural-language prompts into working web apps and websites with built-in database, hosting, and media generation.
- [v0 by Vercel](https://v0.app) — AI tool from Vercel that generates React and Next.js frontend and full-stack code from prompts and can deploy the result as a live site.
- [Webstudio](https://webstudio.is) — Open-source visual website builder with full CSS control, headless CMS connections, and self-hosting options.

## Design-to-Code / Component Generators

Tools that generate UI, components, or convert designs into production frontend code.

- [Anima](https://www.animaapp.com) — AI agent that generates production-ready code and live websites from Figma files, URLs, or prompts.
- [Builder.io](https://www.builder.io) — Visual development platform whose Visual Copilot uses AI to convert Figma designs into framework code and build pages visually.
- [Framer AI](https://www.framer.com) — Visual no-code website builder with hosting and CMS that uses AI to generate page layouts and content from text descriptions.
- [Fronty](https://fronty.com) — AI tool that converts an uploaded image or screenshot of a design into HTML and CSS code, with a no-code editor.
- [Kombai](https://kombai.com) — AI frontend agent that converts Figma designs, images, or instructions into production-ready React, Vue, or Svelte code in the IDE.
- [Locofy](https://www.locofy.ai) — AI design-to-code tool that converts Figma and Penpot designs into responsive frontend code across multiple frameworks.
- [Magic Patterns](https://www.magicpatterns.com) — AI prototyping platform that generates production-ready UI and prototypes matching an existing design system.
- [Plasmic](https://www.plasmic.app) — Visual builder for websites and apps that integrates with existing React codebases and supports AI-generated components.
- [Polymet](https://polymet.ai) — AI product-design tool that generates pages, components, and production-ready frontend code with Figma import and export.
- [Relume](https://www.relume.io) — AI tool that generates website sitemaps and wireframes from a prompt and exports components to Figma, Webflow, or React.
- [Subframe](https://www.subframe.com) — AI-native design-to-code tool that generates pages and components and exports clean React code using Radix UI and Tailwind.
- [TeleportHQ](https://teleporthq.io) — Low-code AI website builder that generates sites from prompts and exports production-ready code for React, Vue, Next.js, and Angular.
- [Uizard](https://uizard.io) — AI-powered UI design tool that generates wireframes, mockups, and prototypes from text prompts, sketches, or screenshots.
- [Webflow AI Site Builder](https://webflow.com/ai-site-builder) — Webflow feature that turns a prompt into an editable multi-page site built on its CSS framework with styles and animations.

## E-commerce Site Builders

AI-assisted builders focused on online stores.

- [Ecwid by Lightspeed](https://www.ecwid.com) — E-commerce platform whose Instant Site builder includes AI tools that generate site sections and write product descriptions.
- [Shopify Magic](https://www.shopify.com/sidekick) — Shopify's AI suite, where Magic generates text and images in admin fields and Sidekick is a conversational assistant that performs commerce tasks in the store admin.
- [Square Online](https://squareup.com/us/en/online-store) — E-commerce site builder and host with AI features that generate site layouts, product descriptions, and marketing copy tied to Square payments and inventory.

## Landing Page Builders

AI-assisted builders focused on landing pages and conversion-oriented pages.

- [Gamma](https://gamma.app/products/websites) — Presentation and content tool whose website feature generates and publishes responsive sites from a prompt, with custom domains and forms.
- [Instapage AI](https://instapage.com/en/features/ai-content-generation) — No-code landing page builder with AI content generation for headlines, body text, CTAs, and page variations.
- [Typedream](https://typedream.com) — No-code AI website builder for creators and small businesses to build sites and sell digital products.
- [Unbounce Smart Builder](https://unbounce.com) — AI-assisted landing page builder that suggests industry-specific layouts and copy and routes traffic to higher-converting variants.
- [Unicorn Platform](https://unicornplatform.com) — Drag-and-drop AI website and landing-page builder for startups, SaaS, and apps, with AI-generated copy and templates.

## Platform and CMS AI Builder Features

AI generation built into a larger hosting, WordPress, or CMS platform.

- [Bluehost WonderSuite](https://www.bluehost.com/wondersuite) — AI WordPress onboarding toolkit that generates a multi-page WordPress site with content, imagery, and layout from plain-English questions.
- [Divi AI](https://www.elegantthemes.com/ai) — AI text, image, and code generator built into the Divi WordPress visual builder.
- [Elementor AI](https://elementor.com/products/ai) — Native AI feature set inside the Elementor WordPress page builder that generates text, code, images, and layout containers from prompts.
- [Hostinger Horizons](https://www.hostinger.com/horizons) — No-code AI builder that generates and deploys functional web apps and sites from chat, voice, or screenshot inputs, with hosting, domain, and database.
- [IONOS AI Website Builder](https://www.ionos.com/websites/ai-website-builder) — Builder that uses AI to generate a branded multi-section site with text, images, and color palettes from a few prompts.
- [10Web](https://10web.io) — AI-powered WordPress website builder and managed-hosting platform that generates production-ready WordPress sites from a prompt and supports Figma import.
- [Tilda AI](https://tilda.cc/lp/ai) — No-code website builder whose AI generates full pages, copy, custom blocks, images, and meta tags from a business description.
- [Web.com AI Website Builder](https://www.web.com/websites/ai-website-builder) — AI builder that asks a few questions, then selects a layout, adds images, and drafts content for a site, store, and marketing pages.
